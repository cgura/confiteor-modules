# Responsibilities to God

- **Category:** CONFESSION_PREP
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Description:** Look back on your life in the light of God’s Word to honestly recognize the sins you’ve committed since your last worthy confession, so you can turn back to God with repentance and be ready to receive His mercy.

### 1
Have I not gone to Mass on Sunday?
> I have not gone to Mass on Sunday.

### 2
Have I rebelled and been stubborn about going to Mass?
> I rebelled and have been stubborn about going to Mass.

### 3
Did I not participate in the Mass or did I daydream?
> I did not participate in the Mass or I daydreamed.

### 4
Have I not prayed every day?
> I have not prayed every day.

### 5
Have I not read the Bible?
> I have not read the Bible.

### 6
Have I been rebellious toward God and his commands?
> I have been rebellious toward God and his commands.

### 7
Have I misused the name of God by swearing and cursing?
> I have misused the name of God by swearing and cursing.

### 8
Have I not told the Father that I love him for creating me and making me his son/daughter?
> I have not told the Father that I love him for creating me and making me his son/daughter.

### 9
Have I not thanked Jesus for becoming man, dying for my sin and rising to give me eternal life?
> I have not thanked Jesus for becoming man, dying for my sin and rising to give me eternal life.

### 10
Have I not asked the Holy Spirit to help me conquer sin and temptation and to be obedient to God’s commands?
> I have not asked the Holy Spirit to help me conquer sin and temptation and to be obedient to God’s commands.

# Responsibilities to others and myself

- **Category:** CONFESSION_PREP
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Description:** Look back with a sincere effort (in the light of God’s Word) to recognize how your sins harmed other people and how they damaged your own true self and relationship with God, so you can confess them with repentance.

### 1
Have I been rebellious, disobedient or disrespectful to my parents, teachers and those in authority over me?
> I have been rebellious, disobedient or disrespectful to my parents, teachers and those in authority over me.

### 2
Have I lied to or deceived my parents or others?
> I have lied to or deceived my parents or others.

### 3
Have I been arrogant and stubborn?
> I have been arrogant and stubborn.

### 4
Have I talked back to my parents or those in authority?
> I have talked back to my parents or those in authority.

### 5
Have I gotten angry or nurtured and held grudges and resentments?
> I have gotten angry or nurtured and held grudges and resentments.

### 6
Have I refused to forgive others?
> I have refused to forgive others.

### 7
Have I cultivated hatred?
> I have cultivated hatred.

### 8
Have I engaged in sexual fantasies?
> I have engaged in sexual fantasies.

### 9
Have I looked at others lustfully?
> I have looked at others lustfully.

### 10
Have I read pornographic literature or looked at pornographic pictures, shows or movies?
> I have read pornographic literature or looked at pornographic pictures, shows or movies.

### 11
Have I masturbated?
> I have masturbated.

### 12
Have I lustfully kissed or sexually touched someone?
> I have lustfully kissed or sexually touched someone.

### 13
Have I had sexual intercourse?
> I have had sexual intercourse.

### 14
Have I had an abortion or encouraged another to have one?
> I have had an abortion or encouraged another to have one.

### 15
Have I gossiped about others?
> I have gossiped about others.

### 16
Have I slandered anyone?
> I have slandered anyone.

### 17
Have I told lies about others?
> I have told lies about others.

### 18
Have I mocked or made fun of others?
> I have mocked or made fun of others.

### 19
Have I lied or cheated?
> I have lied or cheated.

### 20
Have I stolen anything?
> I have stolen anything.

### 21
Have I paid it back?
> I have paid it back.

### 22
Have I been selfish or spiteful toward others?
> I have been selfish or spiteful toward others.

### 23
Have I been jealous?
> I have been jealous.

### 24
Have I gotten drunk, or taken drugs?
> I have gotten drunk, or taken drugs.

### 25
Have I participated in anything that is of the occult: ouija boards, fortune tellers, séances, channeling, astrology?
> I have participated in anything that is of the occult: ouija boards, fortune tellers, séances, channeling, astrology.

### 26
Have I been patient, kind gentle and self-controlled?
> I have been patient, kind gentle and self-controlled.

### 27
When my conscience told me to do something good, did I do it or did I ignore it?
> I When my conscience told me to do something good, did I do it or did I ignore it.
