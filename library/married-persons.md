# Responsibilities to God

- **Category:** CONFESSION_PREP
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Description:** Evaluate how faithfully you live your Baptism and sacramental marriage as a “community at prayer and dialogue with God,” seeking to grow in faith so the Gospel radiates through your everyday life.

> I have gone to Mass every Sunday.

### 4

### 5
