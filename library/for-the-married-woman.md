# Pride

- **Category:** SEVEN_CAPITAL_SINS
- **Framework:** CAPITAL_SINS
- **Description:** an excessive love of self or the desire to be better or more important than others

### 1
Do I have a superior attitude because I am married or because I’ve been married longer than someone else?
> I have a superior attitude because I am married or because I’ve been married longer than someone else.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED

### 2
Do I think my marriage is better than someone else’s?
> I think my marriage is better than someone else’s.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED

### 3
Do I not honor my husband as the spiritual head of our family?
> I don't honor my husband as the spiritual head of our family.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED

### 4
Do I speak poorly of my husband to his face or in front of others?
> I speak poorly of my husband to his face or in front of others.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED

### 5
Am I not charitable to members of my family of origin?
> I am not charitable to members of my family of origin.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED

### 6
Am I not charitable to members of his family of origin?
> I am not charitable to members of his family of origin.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED

### 7
Where do I place my individual worth? Do I not humbly accept God’s fatherly love for me?
> I don't humbly accept God’s fatherly love for me.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED
