# God (faith, prayer, reverence)

- **Category:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Scripture:** You shall have no other gods before Me. (Ex 20:3)
- **Description:** Fidelity to God alone—trusting, praying, and living reverently before the Lord.

### 1
I neglected prayer or lived as if God was not important.
> I neglected prayer / lived as if God was not important.

### 2
I showed irreverence toward God, worship, or holy things.
> I showed irreverence toward God / worship.

### 3
I have not been honest about my faith (or I refused to change when I knew I should).
> I refused to change when I knew better.

# Sunday / Mass (the Lord’s Day)

- **Category:** PRECEPTS_OF_THE_CHURCH
- **Framework:** PRECEPTS
- **Scripture:** Remember the Sabbath day, to keep it holy. (Ex 20:8)
- **Description:** Keeping holy time for the Lord through worship and rest.

### 1
I missed Mass on Sunday/Holy Days without a serious reason.
> I missed Mass without a serious reason.

# Truth and trust

- **Category:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Scripture:** You shall not bear false witness against your neighbor. (Ex 20:16)
- **Description:** Living truthfully and protecting others through honest, charity-filled speech.

### 1
I lied, cheated, or broke trust.
> I lied/cheated/broke trust.

### 2
I gossiped or harmed someone’s reputation with untrue or distorted speech.
> I harmed someone’s reputation with my speech.

# Chastity / purity (thoughts, words, actions, habits)

- **Category:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Scripture:** You shall not commit adultery. (Ex 20:14)
- **Description:** Guarding purity and self-mastery—resisting what drags the heart away from holiness.

### 1
I sinned against chastity (including deliberate choices that I knew were wrong).
> I sinned against chastity.

### 2
I repeatedly fed the sin through habits (not just brief temptation).
> I repeatedly fed sinful habits.

# Anger and harm

- **Category:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Scripture:** You shall not murder. (Ex 20:13)
- **Description:** Protecting life and charity—rejecting destructive anger, resentment, and unforgiveness.

### 1
I seriously harmed someone with anger, threats, violence, or serious disrespect.
> I harmed someone with anger/disrespect/harm.

### 2
I refused to forgive, or I kept hatred/revenge in my heart.
> I refused to forgive / kept resentment.

# Theft / fairness

- **Category:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Scripture:** You shall not steal. (Ex 20:15)
- **Description:** Living justly—honoring what is owed and repairing harm when possible.

### 1
I took what was not mine or failed to make things right when I could.
> I took what was not mine / failed to make things right.

# Church responsibilities (simple checks)

- **Category:** PRECEPTS_OF_THE_CHURCH
- **Framework:** PRECEPTS
- **Scripture:** Let us draw near with a sincere heart. (Heb 10:22)
- **Description:** Accountability before God—confessing honestly and receiving the sacraments worthily.

### 1
After serious sin, I delayed confession for a long time (without a real reason).
> I delayed confession after serious sin.

### 2
I neglected confession/communion when I should have.
> I neglected confession/communion.

# Final check (to make confession fruitful)

- **Category:** CONFESSION
- **Framework:** CONFESSION
- **Scripture:** Godly sorrow produces repentance that leads to salvation. (2 Cor 7:10)
- **Description:** Making sure repentance is real—truth, sorrow, intention to change, and confession of grave sins.

### 1
I can say: “I have sinned” (not only “sin exists”).
> I have sinned; I’m responsible.

### 2
I have real sorrow and a real intention to change (not just regret).
> I reject the sin and resolve not to return to it.

### 3
I will confess the grave (mortal) sins I remember that I have not yet confessed before.
> I will confess my unconfessed grave sins.
