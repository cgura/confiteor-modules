# First Commandment — Faith and Worship

- **Category:** COMPREHENSIVE_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Examination Type:** COMPREHENSIVE
- **Description:** Examines faith in God, trust in His mercy, and the rejection of superstition and false worship.

### 1
Have I knowingly rejected or misrepresented the Catholic faith?
> I knowingly rejected or misrepresented the Catholic faith.

### 2
Have I put money, power, pleasure, ambition, or another person ahead of God?
> I put something or someone ahead of God.

### 3
Have I neglected prayer or failed to seek help in addressing serious doubts about the faith?
> I neglected prayer or failed to address serious doubts about the faith.

### 4
Have I taken part in fortune-telling, divination, or occult practices?
> I took part in fortune-telling, divination, or occult practices.

### 5
Have I presumed on God’s mercy by choosing to continue in sin, or despaired of His mercy because of my sins?
> I presumed on God’s mercy or despaired of His mercy.

# Second Commandment — Reverence for God’s Name

- **Category:** COMPREHENSIVE_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Examination Type:** COMPREHENSIVE
- **Description:** Examines whether I honor God’s name, promises, and holy things.

### 1
Have I used God’s name irreverently, cursed, or spoken contemptuously about sacred things?
> I used God’s name irreverently, cursed, or spoke contemptuously about sacred things.

### 2
Have I made a false oath or invoked God’s name to support a lie?
> I made a false oath or invoked God’s name to support a lie.

### 3
Have I broken a serious vow or promise made to God?
> I broke a serious vow or promise made to God.

# Third Commandment — Keeping the Lord’s Day Holy

- **Category:** COMPREHENSIVE_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Examination Type:** COMPREHENSIVE
- **Description:** Examines participation in Sunday worship and respect for the Lord’s Day.

### 1
Have I deliberately missed Sunday Mass or a holy day of obligation without a serious reason?
> I deliberately missed Sunday Mass or a holy day of obligation without a serious reason.

### 2
Have I arrived late for Mass or left early without a serious reason?
> I arrived late for Mass or left early without a serious reason.

### 3
Have I chosen unnecessary work or activities that interfered with keeping the Lord’s Day holy?
> I chose unnecessary work or activities that interfered with keeping the Lord’s Day holy.

# Fourth Commandment — Family and Authority

- **Category:** COMPREHENSIVE_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Examination Type:** COMPREHENSIVE
- **Description:** Examines my duties toward family members and respect for legitimate authority.

### 1
Have I treated my parents with contempt, hatred, or serious disrespect?
> I treated my parents with contempt, hatred, or serious disrespect.

### 2
Have I neglected a family member who reasonably depended on me for care or support?
> I neglected a family member who depended on me for care or support.

### 3
Have I disobeyed legitimate authority or failed to respect just laws?
> I disobeyed legitimate authority or failed to respect just laws.

### 4
If I am a parent, have I neglected my children’s care, education, or religious formation?
> I neglected my children’s care, education, or religious formation.
- **Applicable To:** PARENT

# Fifth Commandment — Respect for Human Life

- **Category:** COMPREHENSIVE_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Examination Type:** COMPREHENSIVE
- **Description:** Examines respect for life, the well-being of others, and the rejection of hatred and violence.

### 1
Have I deliberately harmed, threatened, or seriously abused another person?
> I deliberately harmed, threatened, or seriously abused another person.

### 2
Have I nursed hatred or sought revenge against someone?
> I nursed hatred or sought revenge against someone.

### 3
Have I endangered myself or others through reckless behavior or substance abuse?
> I endangered myself or others through reckless behavior or substance abuse.

### 4
Have I encouraged another person to sin or helped that person do so?
> I encouraged another person to sin or helped that person do so.

# Sixth and Ninth Commandments — Chastity and Fidelity

- **Category:** COMPREHENSIVE_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Examination Type:** COMPREHENSIVE
- **Description:** Examines chastity, fidelity, modesty, and respect for the dignity of others.

### 1
Have I deliberately entertained lustful thoughts or sought sexual pleasure outside the proper context of marriage?
> I deliberately entertained lustful thoughts or sought sexual pleasure outside marriage.

### 2
Have I used pornography or encouraged another person to use it?
> I used pornography or encouraged another person to use it.

### 3
Have I been unfaithful to my spouse or engaged in sexual activity outside marriage?
> I was unfaithful to my spouse or engaged in sexual activity outside marriage.

### 4
Have I pressured, exploited, or made unwelcome sexual advances toward another person?
> I pressured, exploited, or made unwelcome sexual advances toward another person.

### 5
Have I acted against the Church’s teaching on marital intimacy or failed to respect my spouse?
> I acted against the Church’s teaching on marital intimacy or failed to respect my spouse.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED

# Seventh and Tenth Commandments — Justice and Detachment

- **Category:** COMPREHENSIVE_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Examination Type:** COMPREHENSIVE
- **Description:** Examines honesty in the use of property, fairness in dealings, and detachment from material goods.

### 1
Have I stolen, cheated, or deliberately damaged another person’s property?
> I stole, cheated, or deliberately damaged another person’s property.

### 2
Have I failed to repay a debt, honor an agreement, or pay a just wage?
> I failed to repay a debt, honor an agreement, or pay a just wage.

### 3
Have I been greedy, envious, or unwilling to share with people in need?
> I was greedy or envious, or I was unwilling to share with people in need.

### 4
Have I used resources carelessly or wasted what could have helped others?
> I used resources carelessly or wasted what could have helped others.

# Eighth Commandment — Truth and Reputation

- **Category:** COMPREHENSIVE_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Examination Type:** COMPREHENSIVE
- **Description:** Examines truthfulness, respect for others’ reputations, and care in speech.

### 1
Have I lied, deliberately deceived someone, or given false testimony?
> I lied, deliberately deceived someone, or gave false testimony.

### 2
Have I gossiped, unjustly harmed someone’s reputation, or made a rash judgment?
> I gossiped, unjustly harmed someone’s reputation, or made a rash judgment.

### 3
Have I failed to correct a harmful falsehood or repair damage I caused to someone’s reputation?
> I failed to correct a harmful falsehood or repair damage to someone’s reputation.

# Precepts of the Church

- **Category:** COMPREHENSIVE_PRECEPTS
- **Framework:** PRECEPTS_OF_THE_CHURCH
- **Examination Type:** COMPREHENSIVE
- **Description:** Examines my participation in the Church’s sacramental and penitential life and my support for the Church’s mission.

### 1
Have I neglected the Church’s requirement to confess serious sins?
> I neglected the Church’s requirement to confess serious sins.

### 2
Have I failed to receive Holy Communion during the Easter season when required?
> I failed to receive Holy Communion during the Easter season when required.

### 3
Have I failed to observe the Church’s requirements concerning fasting, abstinence, or Friday penance?
> I failed to observe the Church’s requirements concerning fasting, abstinence, or Friday penance.

### 4
Have I failed to support the Church’s material needs according to my means?
> I failed to support the Church’s material needs according to my means.

### 5
Have I knowingly neglected the Church’s laws concerning marriage?
> I knowingly neglected the Church’s laws concerning marriage.

# Marriage — Fidelity and Responsibility

- **Category:** COMPREHENSIVE_MARRIAGE
- **Framework:** MARRIAGE
- **Examination Type:** COMPREHENSIVE
- **Description:** For married persons, examines fidelity, mutual respect, and responsibilities within married life.

### 1
Have I failed to remain faithful to my spouse in thought, word, or action?
> I failed to remain faithful to my spouse in thought, word, or action.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED

### 2
Have I treated my spouse with cruelty, contempt, or persistent disrespect?
> I treated my spouse with cruelty, contempt, or persistent disrespect.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED

### 3
Have I failed to take my responsibilities toward my spouse and family seriously?
> I failed to take my responsibilities toward my spouse and family seriously.
- **Applicable To:** MARRIED
