# Responsibilities to God

- **Category:** CONFESSION_PREP
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Description:** The examination reviews whether you have honored God in worship, prayer, and faith, and whether you have practiced forgiveness and recognized your need for Jesus and his grace.

### 1
Have I not gone to Mass every Sunday?
> I have not gone to Mass every Sunday.

### 2
Have I not participated at Mass or have I daydreamed or have not been present with a blank mind?
> I have not participated at Mass or have I daydreamed or have not been present with a blank mind.

### 3
Have I not prayed every day (15-20 minutes)?
> I have not prayed every day (15-20 minutes).

### 4
Have I not read the Bible?
> I have not read the Bible.

### 5
Have I not studied the truths of our faith and allowed them to become more a part of the way I think and act?
> I have not studied the truths of our faith and allowed them to become more a part of the way I think and act.

### 6
Have I not read any spiritual books or religious literature?
> I have not read any spiritual books or religious literature.

### 7
Have I not told God that I want to love him with my whole heart, mind and strength?
> I have not told God that I want to love him with my whole heart, mind and strength.

### 8
Do I hold any resentments toward God?
> I hold resentments toward God.

### 9
Have I not recognized my need for Jesus and his salvation?
> I have not recognized my need for Jesus and his salvation.

### 10
Have I not asked the Holy Spirit to empower me to live the Christian life?
> I have not asked the Holy Spirit to empower me to live the Christian life.

### 11
Have I not been financially generous to the Church?
> I have not been financially generous to the Church.

### 12
Have I not participated in parish or religious activities?
> I have not participated in parish or religious activities.

### 13
Have I held resentments toward the Church or Church authorities?
> I have held resentments toward the Church or Church authorities.

### 14
Have I not forgiven the Church or Church authorities?
> I have not forgiven the Church or Church authorities.

# Responsibilities to others and to myself

- **Category:** CONFESSION_PREP
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Description:** The examination asks whether you have lived justly and truthfully with others, resisted anger and hatred, and protected chastity and purity of mind, speech, and actions.

### 1
Have I been rebellious, disobedient or disrespectful to anyone in authority?
> I have been rebellious, disobedient or disrespectful to anyone in authority.

### 2
Have I lied to or deceived others—friends, boss, or coworkers?
> I have lied to or deceived others—friends, boss, or coworkers.

### 3
Have I been arrogant and stubborn?
> I have been arrogant and stubborn.

### 4
Have I gotten angry or nurtured and held grudges and resentments?
> I have gotten angry or nurtured and held grudges and resentments.

### 5
Have I refused to forgive others—parents,relatives, employers, former friend, a former spouse?
> I have refused to forgive others—parents,relatives, employers, former friend, a former spouse.

### 6
Have I cultivated hatred?
> I have cultivated hatred.

### 7
Have I felt sorry for myself or nurtured self-pity?
> I have felt sorry for myself or nurtured self-pity.

### 8
Have I engaged in sexual fantasies?
> I have engaged in sexual fantasies.

### 9
Have I looked at others lustfully?
> I have looked at others lustfully.

### 10
Have I read pornographic literature or looked at pornographic pictures, shows or movies?
> I have read pornographic literature or looked at pornographic pictures, shows or movies.

### 11
Have I masturbated?

### 12

> I have lustfully kissed or sexually touched someone.

### 13
Have I had sexual intercourse?

### 14

> I have had an abortion or encouraged another to have one.

### 15

### 16

# Responsibilities to society

- **Category:** CONFESSION_PREP
- **Framework:** TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Description:** The examination considers whether your life has been a witness to the Gospel through justice, charity to the needy, fulfillment of civic duties, and rejection of prejudice and hatred.

### 1
Have I been a Christian witness to those with whom I work or associate?
> I have been a Christian witness to those with whom I work or associate.

### 2
Have I spoken to anyone about the Gospel and how important it is to believe in Jesus?
> I have spoken to anyone about the Gospel and how important it is to believe in Jesus.

### 3
Have I allowed the Gospel to influence my political and social opinions?
> I have allowed the Gospel to influence my political and social opinions.

### 4
Have I had a proper Christian concern for the poor and needy?
> I have had a proper Christian concern for the poor and needy.

### 5
Have I paid my taxes?
> I have paid my taxes.

### 6
Have I fostered or nurtured hatred toward my ‘political’ opponents, either local, national or international?
> I have fostered or nurtured hatred toward my ‘political’ opponents, either local, national or international.

### 7
Have I been prejudiced toward others because of race, color, religion or social status?
> I have been prejudiced toward others because of race, color, religion or social status.
