# First Commandment

- **Category:** CHILDREN_TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Framework:** CHILDREN_TEN_COMMANDMENTS
- **Examination Type:** STANDARD
- **Scripture:** You shall have no other gods before Me. (Ex 20:3)
- **Description:** Loving God above all things and trusting in Him completely.

### 1
Have I loved God with all my heart?
> I did not love God with all my heart.

### 2
Have I forgotten to pray?
> I forgot to pray.

### 3
Have I trusted in God when I was scared or worried?
> I did not trust in God when I was scared or worried.

### 4
Have I put other things, like toys or games, before God?
> I put other things, like toys or games, before God.
